01 — About me

The person behind the production.

I’m Mohamed Chalaby, an Egyptian producer working across commercial advertising, branded campaigns, and digital entertainment.

I believe great productions start with great ideas and the right people to bring them to life. From planning and coordinating crews to managing logistics and overseeing on-set execution, I take pride in turning creative concepts into impactful productions.

Alongside my work in commercial advertising, I’m responsible for producing digital content for El Adl Group Digital, bringing together storytelling, creativity, and production expertise to create engaging entertainment.
